# Sources and intended use

- [Cambridge International AS & A Level Chemistry 9701 syllabus, 2025–2027](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/664563-2025-2027-syllabus.pdf) — scope anchor for the Round 1 content families; this skill does not reproduce syllabus text.
- [Cambridge 9701 qualification page](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-international-as-and-a-level-chemistry-9701/) — current syllabus landing page and revision check.
- [IUPAC Gold Book: systematic name](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/S06236) and [locant](https://goldbook.iupac.org/terms/view/09014) — terminology anchors.
- [IUPAC Brief Guide to Organic Nomenclature](https://iupac.qmul.ac.uk/BriefGuide/organic.html) — background nomenclature conventions.

The references guide recognition and review only. They never permit inventing chemistry absent from speech or a case’s approved context.
