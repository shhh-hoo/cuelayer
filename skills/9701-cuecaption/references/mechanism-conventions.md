# Mechanism language

Recognise 9701 mechanism vocabulary in speech: electrophile, nucleophile, heterolytic fission, homolytic fission, curly arrow, radical, intermediate, activation energy, rate-determining step, and electron pair.

Keep these as words in `canonicalText`. A `mechanism-term` or `causal-link` hint can make an existing meaning salient, but does not construct a mechanism.

Do not infer arrow origin/destination, electron counts, species charges, intermediates, or products from a named mechanism alone. For example, “electrophilic addition” does not justify adding `HBr`, a carbocation, or a product.
