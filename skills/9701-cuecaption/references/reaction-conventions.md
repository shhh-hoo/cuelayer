# Equations and reactions

Caption the asserted reaction or transformation; do not solve, balance, complete, or repair it.

- Preserve an explicitly spoken reagent, catalyst, solvent, condition, product, coefficient, state symbol, or reversibility marker.
- `equation`, `reaction-arrow`, `condition`, and `product-comparison` may be render hints, not invitations to fill missing notation.
- “It reacts with bromine water” does not license a displayed product unless one was stated or supplied in approved context.
- “On heating” may be preserved as a condition. Do not add `Δ` to Plain text; it may be an FX rewrite if it means only “on heating”.
- If speaker and approved context conflict, preserve the speaker claim and report the conflict. Do not silently correct it.
- A mechanism cue such as “the curly arrow goes here” is not a reaction equation, reagent, or product specification.
