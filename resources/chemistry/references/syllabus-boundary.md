# 9701 boundary

This skill serves Cambridge International AS & A Level Chemistry 9701 explanations across organic, inorganic, physical, analytical, and practical language. It supports caption semantics and optional equivalent FX notation only.

In scope: the teacher’s declared chemistry language, conventional symbols, formulae, reaction statements, mechanism terms, experimental observations, cautions, and exam-style naming discussion.

Out of scope: ASR, a knowledge base, caption composer, AI effect planner, PPT integration, diagram or molecular rendering, a reaction predictor, a mechanism generator, chemical error correction, and full chemistry parsing.

The skill does not broaden the current product or renderer’s scope.
